Kalm Corner is a cozy little corner for when you need to slow down. Three gentle puzzle games, soft sounds and a moonlit sky. No timers, no pressure, just you and a quiet evening.

Pour soft colours from glass to glass until every one is calm and whole. A soothing colour-sort puzzle with undo, hints and an extra tube when you need one.

Turn the stepping stones so the spring can reach every flower. A relaxing flow puzzle that grows from a tiny garden to a sprawling moonlit pond.

🪟 CLEAR WINDOW
Wipe away fog, rain, frost and mud with your finger and watch the view come back: lighthouses, hills, quiet streets and sleepy skies.

• 156 handcrafted levels across cozy chapters
• No timers, no fail states. Play at your own pace
• Gentle music and soft, satisfying sound effects
• Light haptics that feel just right (or turn them off)
• Day, Evening and Night colour themes
• Colour-blind symbols and reduce-motion options
• Works fully offline
