## Title
Calm Corner: Relaxing Games

## Short description (<= 80 chars)
Calm little games: wipe windows, pop bubbles, rake sand.

## Long description
Calm Corner is a pocket of small, satisfying things to do when you need a breath. Wipe a foggy window and watch the world come back. Pop soft bubbles. Rake a sand garden and place stones. Pour colors until every tube is one. Water a plant and watch it bloom. No scores, no timers, no losing: just gentle touch, soft sound and a little calm. Works offline.
