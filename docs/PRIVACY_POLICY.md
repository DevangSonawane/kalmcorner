# Privacy Policy (draft)

Calm Corner does not collect personal data in the MVP build.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics or crash reporting unless you opt in after enabling the optional Phase 5 services.
- Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device (SharedPreferences) and never leave it.
- Audio and haptics are generated locally; no network calls are made by the app.
- If you enable optional services (Firebase/AdMob/RevenueCat), their respective privacy policies apply and a consent flow is shown where required.
